## Literature guard

Xiao et al. (2014) tested the same 176-instance benchmark and reports new best-known solutions
for 5/40 medium and 30/40 large instances. Those paper values are not injected until the
per-instance table is reconciled to the canonical ph2/ph3 identifiers.

Phase 1 is different: the literature confirms that optimal solutions are known for all 96 small instances.
A repository result is nevertheless labelled PROVEN_OPTIMAL only when the exact local result carries
corresponding optimality evidence.
