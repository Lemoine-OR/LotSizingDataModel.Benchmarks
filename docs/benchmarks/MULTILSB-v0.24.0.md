# MULTILSB official acquisition - v0.24.0

The official Akartunali-Miller archive is preserved under `benchmarks/MULTILSB/raw`.

- Source: University of Strathclyde Pure
- DOI: 10.15129/252b7827-b62b-4af4-8869-64b12b1c69a1
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Corpus: four sets of thirty instances (120 total)
- Evidence: LP relaxation bounds and heuristic solutions are present in the original workbook
- MIPLIB 2010 source-declared overlap: set3-10, set3-15, set3-20

No canonical XML is created in this release. MULTILSB contains explicit backlogging semantics and requires a lossless mapping into LotSizingDataModel before admission. The LP bounds are not optimality certificates, and heuristic objective values are not promoted to proven optima.
