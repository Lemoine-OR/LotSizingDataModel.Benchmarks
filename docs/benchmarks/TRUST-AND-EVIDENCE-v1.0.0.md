# Trust and evidence - v1.0.0

The normalized trust taxonomy is evidence-based and deliberately conservative. The final catalogue preserves 7,684 records with no known reference, 120 literature best-reported values, 96 Checker-verified feasible solutions, and five lower-bound references requiring reconciliation.

The 125 actionable challenges are not synthetic placeholders: 120 concern acquisition and Checker validation of complete Cattrysse solutions, while five concern reconciliation of Trigeiro lower-bound claims. No record is promoted to proven optimal without a complete verified solution and sufficient proof.
