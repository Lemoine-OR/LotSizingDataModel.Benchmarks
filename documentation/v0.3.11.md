# v0.3.11 — real datab/B+ importer repair

The official CLSPL website states that `datab` contains 60 MLCLSP instances taken from
Stadtler class B+.

The extracted `datab` directories do not contain the explicit CLSPL files `KAPAZ.PRN`,
`LAGKOST.PRN` or `RUESTK.PRN`. Instead they contain the Stadtler generation variables
`AUSLAST.PRN`, `MITT_BED.PRN`, `TBO.PRN` and `ZFKOEF.PRN`.

The repaired converter therefore detects this source contract and reconstructs:

- mean net demand from mean primary demand and the BOM;
- capacities from resource utilization, production coefficients, setup times and TBO;
- holding costs from marginal holding costs and the BOM;
- setup costs from marginal holding cost, TBO and mean net demand.

These are the generation relationships documented for the official Stadtler MLCLSP test
instances.

The resulting XML remains tagged as `SUERIE_CLSPL / CLSPL`, with explicit provenance that
the source is `datab`, a Stadtler B+ derivative.

`FLAGS.PRN`, `YFIX.PRN` and `ZFIX.PRN` are retained as raw provenance and are not silently
interpreted as core benchmark coefficients because they are not part of the published
11-file CLSPL instance contract.

Reference objective values and lower bounds from `solb.xls` remain literature evidence,
not verified solutions.
